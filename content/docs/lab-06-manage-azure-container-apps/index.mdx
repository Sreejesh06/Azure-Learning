## Overview

![Mastering Azure Container Apps](https://i0.wp.com/rajanieshkaushikk.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/mastering-azure-container-apps-config-to-deployments.jpg)

In this lab, you will explore how to manage containers in Azure Container Apps. This includes managing container apps across their day-two lifecycle, such as updating images, managing revisions, diagnosing failing deployments, tuning resources and scaling, and troubleshooting with logs and health probes.

## Lab Modules

In this lab module, you will learn the fundamental operations required for ongoing management of containerized workloads efficiently.
